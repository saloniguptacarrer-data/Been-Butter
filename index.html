A small café built around long chats

Bean & Butter began as an imagined corner in a busy market: a place where the coffee is roasted in small batches, the bread is shaped by hand at dawn, and nobody rushes you out.

We buy vegetables, eggs and dairy from nearby farms, keep the menu short so everything stays fresh, and leave the tables a little too close together, so conversations tend to wander.

This story is fictional and written for a demo website.
